# Month-End Closing Procedure (Finance)

## Closing timeline
Bank reconciliations are due by the 2nd working day, accruals by the 3rd working day, and the books must be closed by the 5th working day of the following month.

## Journal entry approval
Manual journal entries above IDR 250,000,000 need CFO approval before posting.

## Sign-off
The financial controller signs off the closing checklist and sends the management report to the CFO.
