# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Permanent employees receive 14 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, available after the 3-month probation period. Annual leave longer than 2 consecutive days must be requested in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance and approved by your direct manager.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March and cannot be paid out in cash.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is paid. Absences longer than 2 consecutive days require a doctor's note uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work.

## Parental leave
Mothers receive 3 months of paid maternity leave. Fathers receive 3 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the birth.
