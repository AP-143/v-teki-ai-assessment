# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
After 3 months of employment, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Core hours
Everyone must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 Western Indonesia Time (WIB), on remote days.

## Home office allowance
Employees receive a one-time home office allowance of IDR 1,500,000 every 2 years for a chair, monitor or similar equipment.

## Working abroad
Working from another country needs prior HR approval and is limited to 14 days per calendar year.
