# Procurement Approval Policy

## Approval limits
Purchases up to IDR 5,000,000 need approval from your manager. Purchases above IDR 5,000,000 up to IDR 50,000,000 need approval from the department head and 2 supplier quotes. Purchases above IDR 50,000,000 need CFO approval, 3 supplier quotes and a procurement committee review.

## Suppliers
Use suppliers from the preferred vendor list. A new supplier must pass the vendor onboarding check before the first order.

## Splitting purchases
Splitting one purchase into smaller orders to stay under an approval limit is not allowed and is treated as a policy violation.
