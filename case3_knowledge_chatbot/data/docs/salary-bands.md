# Salary Bands (Confidential)

## Monthly salary bands
Level 1: IDR 6,000,000 to 9,000,000. Level 2: IDR 9,000,000 to 14,000,000. Level 3: IDR 14,000,000 to 22,000,000. Level 4: IDR 22,000,000 to 35,000,000. Level 5: IDR 35,000,000 to 55,000,000.

## Annual salary review
Salaries are reviewed every March. The average increase budget for 2027 is 6 percent of the total payroll.

## Confidentiality
Band data may be shared only with HR staff and the employee's own line manager.
