# Business Travel and Reimbursement

## Submitting a claim
Submit travel expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days after the trip ends. Receipts are required for every expense above IDR 100,000. Approved claims are paid within 14 working days.

## Daily allowance
The daily allowance (per diem) is IDR 450,000 per day for domestic trips and USD 60 per day for international trips. It covers meals and local transport.

## Flights
Book economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours and needs written approval from a director.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is IDR 1,200,000 per night in Jakarta and IDR 900,000 per night in other cities. Book through the company travel agent whenever possible.
